WHAT MIMULUS IS

An explorer for interpretation under change.

Decode boldly

Mimulus can try structural, symbolic, linguistic and adversarial readings. Speculation is allowed in discovery mode; the route that produced it is never erased.

Translate usefully

Translations are first-class outputs. They are labelled by lineage so a fluent downstream translation is not mistaken for independent evidence about the original source.

Change one thing

Branching lets you swap a decoder, framing, representation or history while holding the source fixed. That turns “maybe” into something testable.

Watch what survives

Mimulus is most interested in structure that persists across meaningful changes — and equally interested when nothing survives.

Boundary

Mimulus does not turn coherence into truth. A compelling decode or translation is an experimental reading until independently supported from the source and external evidence.
